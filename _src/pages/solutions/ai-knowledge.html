how it works

Index, retrieve, answer, cite.

Documents are chunked and indexed; each question retrieves the most relevant passages; the model answers only from those and shows them. Permissions follow your existing access rules.

01

Connect sources

SharePoint, Drive, Odoo Documents and Knowledge, ticket systems, PDFs.

02

Index with permissions

Who can see a document decides who can get an answer from it.

03

Answer with citations

Every answer links to the passages it used; "I don't know" when the documents don't say.

04

Improve from feedback

Thumbs up/down and unanswered questions feed a monthly review of gaps in the documents.
