editions and hosting

Where Odoo runs is a scoping decision, not a default.

{{icon:globe}}

Odoo Online

Odoo's own cloud. Standard apps, automatic upgrades, fastest to start. Best when no custom modules are needed.

{{icon:code}}

Odoo.sh

Odoo's platform for custom development — staging branches, backups and version control included. Best when you need tailored modules.

{{icon:lock}}

On-premise / own cloud

Your servers or your cloud account. Best when data residency or existing infrastructure decides it.
