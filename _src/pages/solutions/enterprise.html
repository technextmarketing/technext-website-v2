Multi-country

Localisations per country — chart of accounts, tax, e-invoicing — with group reporting in one currency.

A sale in one entity creates the purchase in the other; invoices and bills match automatically.

Approval chains, record rules and audit trails; segregation of duties enforced by configuration.

Group P&L, balance sheet and cash on demand — eliminations handled, not typed.
